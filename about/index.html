How a booking actually runs

  1. You call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203 with pickup point, destination, date, time and passenger count.
  2. The desk checks live vehicle availability and sends a fixed written quote that separates fare from tolls, permits, parking and any waiting.
  3. You confirm with a small advance by UPI, bank transfer or cash at the counter.
  4. Driver name, vehicle number and live location reach you before the pickup window — the evening before for pre-dawn airport runs.
  5. The trip closes with a line-item trip sheet and receipts for any actuals. Corporate accounts are consolidated monthly.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish invented testimonials, star ratings or review markup. Rider feedback lives on our Google Business Profile, where each review carries a real account and date.
  • We do not apply surge pricing during rain, peak hours or festival weeks.
  • We do not bundle tolls and permits into a padded package estimate; they are billed at actuals with receipts.
  • We do not hand trips to unverified drivers outside our roster.
  • We do not offer discounts or incentives in exchange for ratings.
