Four steps from message to confirmed car

  1. Send pickup point, drop point, date and time, passenger count and luggage on WhatsApp.
  2. The desk checks live availability and replies with a fixed written quote — fare, tolls, permits, parking and waiting shown separately.
  3. Confirm with a small advance by UPI or bank transfer. The vehicle is locked and the driver assigned.
  4. Driver name, vehicle number and live location reach you before the pickup window, with the trip sheet closed at the end.

What to include in your first message

  • Pickup address with a landmark, and the drop address or city
  • Date and time, plus whether the pickup time is fixed or flexible
  • Passenger count and luggage volume — a family of six with four suitcases needs an SUV, not a sedan
  • Trip type: one-way drop, round trip, full-day local, airport transfer or multi-day circuit
  • Any mobility needs, child seat requirement or extra stop

Advance, balance and cancellation

  • A small advance confirms the booking; the balance is payable at the end of the trip in cash, UPI or bank transfer.
  • Corporate accounts can move to monthly consolidated invoicing after the first few runs.
  • Cancellation terms depend on trip type: local and airport trips can be cancelled free up to four hours before pickup, outstation up to 24 hours before.
  • The written quote carries the applicable cancellation terms for your specific trip.
  • Refunds are returned by the original payment method once the trip record is checked.

Corporate, wedding and group bookings

These need a conversation rather than a form. Employee transport rosters, multi-vehicle wedding grids and monthly car contracts each get a named coordinator on WhatsApp for the duration of the engagement, with a single consolidated invoice.

  • Corporate: pickup grid, shift timings and headcount for a route survey
  • Wedding: function dates, venues and guest count for a vehicle-wise roster
  • Group travel: destination, dates and group size for Urbania or Tempo Traveller allocation
  • Outstation: full itinerary for a day-by-day quote with night halts itemised

Frequently asked questions

Can I book without paying an advance?

Same-day local runs can sometimes be confirmed on the phone without an advance, subject to availability. Pre-dawn airport pickups, outstation trips and multi-vehicle bookings are confirmed with a small advance so the vehicle is held.

How do I pay the balance?

Cash, UPI or bank transfer at the end of the trip. Corporate accounts can move to monthly invoicing once the account is set up.

Can I change my pickup time after booking?

Yes, subject to vehicle availability. Message the desk as early as possible; changes inside the free-cancellation window are usually accommodated without charge.

What if I need to cancel?

Local and airport trips can be cancelled free up to four hours before pickup, outstation trips up to 24 hours before. Inside those windows the advance is adjusted against actual dispatch costs, and the terms in your written quote govern.

Prefer to talk it through?

Call the dispatch desk with your route and timing. Availability is checked live and the quote is sent in writing.
