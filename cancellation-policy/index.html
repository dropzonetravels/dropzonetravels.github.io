Cancellation and Refund Policy

How to cancel

  1. Call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203 from the number used for the booking.
  2. State the booking name, trip date and route so the record can be located.
  3. The desk confirms the cancellation in writing and states any deduction with its basis.
  4. Any refundable amount is processed to the original payment method.

Refund processing

  • UPI and bank transfers are usually returned within 5–7 working days of confirmation.
  • Cash advances are refunded at the counter or by transfer, whichever you prefer.
  • Refunds are made to the original payer unless you instruct otherwise in writing.
  • A refund reference is shared on WhatsApp so you have a record.

When we cancel

If we cannot provide the confirmed vehicle or driver — because of a breakdown, a roster failure or a safety issue — you get a replacement of the same class at no extra cost, or a full refund of the amount paid, including the advance.

  • A replacement vehicle is the first option, offered before any refund.
  • If no replacement is available within a reasonable time, the unused fare is refunded in full.
  • Waiting time caused by our failure is not billed.
  • For prepaid airport pickups, a missed pickup caused by us is refunded in full.

Weather, road closures and restrictions

Hill routes, monsoon flooding and government restrictions can close roads without notice. Where a trip cannot safely proceed, the booking is rescheduled without a cancellation penalty, or refunded minus any cost already incurred on the ground.

  • Reschedule first — most weather cancellations become trips a day or two later.
  • Permits or entry taxes already paid are refunded only where the authority refunds them.
  • A driver already en route may claim the actual fuel and time cost, shown on the trip sheet.

No-shows

A no-show is treated as a cancellation at the pickup time. If the driver waited at the pickup point and the passenger could not be reached on the booking number, the advance is retained against the dispatch cost. If the driver was delayed or at the wrong point, it is not a no-show — the desk checks the location log before deciding.

Disputes

If you disagree with a deduction, ask for the trip sheet and the basis in writing. Billing disputes are reviewed against the trip record within one working day. Call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203 with the booking name and date.

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel a local taxi booking for free?

Yes, up to four hours before the pickup time. Inside four hours, the advance is adjusted against the actual dispatch cost, which the desk states in writing.

How long does a refund take?

UPI and bank transfer refunds usually reach you within 5–7 working days of the cancellation being confirmed. Cash advances can be refunded at the counter.

What if the weather cancels my hill trip?

Reschedule first. If the trip cannot be moved, it is refunded minus any cost already incurred, such as a permit already paid to the authority.

What counts as a no-show?

The driver waited at the agreed pickup point and could not reach you on the booking number. If the driver was late or at the wrong point, it is not treated as a no-show.

Need to change or cancel a trip?

Message the desk with the booking name and date. You will get a written confirmation and any deduction explained.
