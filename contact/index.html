How to reach the Tigri Extension counter

The office sits on Mangal Bazaar Road in Block A of Tigri Extension, inside the Sangam Vihar grid. It is a short drive from Deoli, Khanpur and Dakshinpuri, and reachable from Saket and Malviya Nagar via the Press Enclave Road and Chirag Delhi links.

  • Landmark: Mangal Bazaar Road, Block A, Tigri Extension — look for the D⚡Z board
  • Nearest practical metro: Govindpuri on the Violet Line, about 7 km away; Saket on the Yellow Line for the northern side
  • From IGI Airport: about 24 km, typically 45–60 minutes outside peak hours
  • Parking: street-side parking on Mangal Bazaar Road; the driver can also meet you at the gate
  • Accessibility: call ahead if you need step-free access or assistance with luggage

Airport pickups at IGI Terminal 1, 2 and 3

For arrivals, the driver tracks your flight and meets you at a pre-agreed pillar in the terminal you land at. Domestic and international arrivals both include a 60-minute free waiting buffer after landing; beyond that, waiting is billed hourly at the rate stated in your written quote.

  • Terminal 3 international: meet at the pillar shared on WhatsApp before you land
  • Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 domestic: arrivals pickup zone confirmed per airline and gate
  • Luggage: the driver assists to the car; trolley help can be arranged for senior travellers
  • Delayed flights: the pickup window moves with the flight, not the clock

Corporate, wedding and monthly desks

Three requirements need a short conversation rather than an instant quote: employee transport rosters, multi-vehicle wedding grids and monthly car contracts. Each gets one named coordinator on WhatsApp for the duration of the engagement.

  • Corporate: share pickup grid, shift timings and headcount for a route survey and monthly quote
  • Weddings: share function dates, venues and guest count for a vehicle-wise roster
  • Monthly car: share the daily route, waiting windows and regular stops for a fixed monthly price
  • Invoicing: monthly consolidated statement with trip logs and receipts attached

What happens after you message

  1. The desk reads your pickup, drop, date and time, and checks live vehicle availability.
  2. You receive a fixed written quote with fare, tolls, permits, parking and any waiting shown separately.
  3. On confirmation, a small advance locks the vehicle and the driver is assigned.
  4. Driver name, vehicle number and live location reach you before the pickup window.
  5. After the trip, the trip sheet and any receipts are shared for your records.

Payments and receipts

  • Cash, UPI and bank transfer accepted on every trip
  • Corporate accounts can move to monthly consolidated invoicing after the first few runs
  • Every trip closes with a line-item trip sheet: fare, tolls, permits, parking, waiting
  • Receipts for tolls and parking are attached — no lump-sum 'miscellaneous' lines
