Editorial Policy – How Our Route Guides Are Produced

Drop Zone Travels · A-357, Mangal Bazaar Rd, Block A, Tigri Extension, Sangam Vihar, New Delhi, Delhi 110080 · +91 72106 69203

Last updated: 15 November 2026. This site carries 127 route and fare guides covering South Delhi neighbourhoods, IGI Airport, outstation corridors, pilgrimage circuits, corporate transport and inbound travel.

This page explains who writes them, where the facts come from, how often they are updated, and how to get something corrected.

This policy describes how content is produced today. It is reviewed when the publishing process changes.

Who writes the guides

Guides are produced by the Drop Zone Travels dispatch desk — the same team that quotes trips, assigns drivers and reconciles toll receipts. That is deliberate: the person writing the fare example is the person who sees the actual bill at the end of the week.

  • Route notes come from driver logs and dispatcher experience on the corridor
  • Fare examples come from the published rate card in site-config, not from promotional pricing
  • Toll estimates come from receipts and current plaza rates, and are labelled as approximate
  • Local detail — gates, parking, departure windows — is reviewed against what drivers report

Sourcing and verification

  • Distances are road distances for the route described, rounded to the corridor figure used in quotes.
  • Fare arithmetic is generated from the rate card so a page cannot show a rate the desk does not honour.
  • Business details — address, phone, hours — are pulled from a single configuration file used by every page.
  • Claims about the company are limited to what can be evidenced: a staffed counter, a defined fleet, a published rate card and 24x7 dispatch.
  • We do not publish invented review counts, passenger numbers, awards or certifications.

Use of AI assistance

Drafting is assisted by software tools, including language models, for structure and first drafts. Every published page is reviewed by the dispatch desk before it goes live, fare figures are calculated by the build script rather than typed by hand, and operational claims are checked against the rate card and driver logs.

  • AI tools do not invent reviews, statistics or certifications for this site
  • Fare and distance tables are generated programmatically
  • A human at the desk signs off route-specific operational detail
  • Errors found after publication are corrected on the page and dated

Independence and advertising

  • No paid placements inside guides and no sponsored route pages
  • No affiliate links to booking platforms, hotels or tour sellers
  • No payment accepted in exchange for a favourable mention
  • Service pages exist to sell our own services and are labelled as such, not disguised as editorial

Corrections

If a fare, distance, toll or operational detail is wrong, tell the desk on +91 72106 69203 with the page and the correction. Verified errors are fixed on the page, and the updated date changes. Substantive corrections — a wrong fare or a closed route — are noted in the page's update line rather than quietly removed.

  1. Send the page URL and what is wrong, with a source if you have one.
  2. The desk checks the rate card, toll receipt or driver log for the corridor.
  3. The page is corrected and the updated date is changed.
  4. For a factual dispute, you get a written reply explaining what was changed or why it stands.
