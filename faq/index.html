Taxi Questions Answered – Fares, Booking & Airport Pickups

Thirty answers to the questions our dispatch desk handles most often, grouped by what you are trying to decide: what it costs, how booking works, airport logistics, outstation planning and payments.

If your question is not here, call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203. The desk answers on the same number 24x7 and will give you a written answer you can keep.

Fares and what is included

  • Sedan ₹14/km · SUV ₹24/km · Urbania or Tempo Traveller ₹32/km. Full-day local packages: ₹2,500, ₹4,500 and ₹7,500 for 8 hours and 80 km.
  • One-way trips are billed on one-way distance. There is no padded return leg and no night surcharge.
  • Tolls, state permits, entry taxes and parking are billed at actuals with receipts, separate from the per-km fare.
  • Airport pickups include a 60-minute free waiting buffer after landing; additional waiting is billed hourly at the quoted rate.
  • Multi-day outstation trips include a driver night-halt allowance, shown in the written quote before departure.
  • The written quote is the final price for the trip described. Changes to route, stops or dates are re-quoted before they are driven.
  • No surge pricing applies during rain, peak hours, festivals or late nights.

Booking and dispatch

  • Book by phone or WhatsApp on +91 72106 69203. Send pickup, drop, date, time and passenger count.
  • A small advance confirms the vehicle; the balance is payable in cash, UPI or bank transfer at the end of the trip.
  • Driver name, vehicle number and live location are shared before the pickup window — the evening before for pre-dawn runs.
  • Pickups inside Sangam Vihar are usually assigned within 15–20 minutes, subject to live availability. Pre-booked slots take priority over on-demand requests.
  • Same-day bookings are best made by phone so availability can be checked while you are on the line.
  • Dispatch operates 24x7 by phone and WhatsApp; the walk-in counter is open 8 AM–10 PM daily.

Airport transfers

  • All three IGI terminals are covered: Terminal 1, Terminal 2 and Terminal 3.
  • The driver tracks your flight and adjusts the pickup time to the actual landing, not the scheduled one.
  • For departures, plan three hours before a domestic flight and four hours before an international flight from South Delhi.
  • Meeting points are shared on WhatsApp before you land, with the driver's number so you can call from the arrivals hall.
  • Airport parking and entry charges are billed at actuals with the receipt attached to your trip sheet.
  • Return transfers can be booked with the same desk at the same time as the outbound leg, so both ends are locked.

Outstation and pilgrimage planning

  • One-way and round-trip outstation quotes are both available; compare them before deciding, because round trips often price better per km.
  • Overnight halts for the driver are part of multi-day quotes and are shown as a separate line, never hidden in the per-km rate.
  • Hill routes such as Shimla, Manali and Nainital are assigned to SUVs or Tempo Travellers with ghat-experienced drivers.
  • Pilgrimage circuits are paced around darshan windows and senior-friendly halts: Vaishno Devi, Salasar, Khatu Shyam, Char Dham, Ayodhya.
  • State permits for interstate travel are arranged by the desk and billed at actuals.
  • For multi-city itineraries, send the full plan and the desk will quote it day by day.

Payments, cancellation and safety

  • Cash, UPI and bank transfer are accepted. Corporate accounts can move to monthly consolidated invoicing.
  • Cancellation terms depend on the trip type; the policy attached to your written quote governs.
  • Every trip closes with a line-item trip sheet showing fare, tolls, permits, parking and waiting, with receipts.
  • Drivers are background-checked and on a known roster. Vehicles are GPS-tracked for the duration of the trip.
  • If a vehicle breaks down, a replacement is dispatched and breakdown waiting time is never re-billed.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Sangam Vihar to IGI Airport?

About 24 km to Terminal 3. A sedan is roughly ₹336 at ₹14/km, an SUV ₹576 at ₹24/km and an Urbania or Tempo Traveller ₹768 at ₹32/km, plus airport parking at actuals. Confirm the exact slot on +91 72106 69203.

Is there a night charge for late or early trips?

No. The rate card is the same at 3 AM as at 3 PM. Pre-dawn airport runs are billed at the published per-km rate with no night surcharge and no surge multiplier.

How are outstation fares calculated?

Fare = class rate × distance. A 280 km Jaipur one-way in a sedan is 280 × ₹14 = ₹3,920. Tolls, permits, parking and any driver night-halt are added at actuals, itemised in the written quote.

Are tolls included in the quoted fare?

No. Tolls, state permits, entry taxes and parking are billed separately at actuals with receipts. This keeps the per-km rate honest instead of padding a package estimate.

What does a full-day local package include?

Eight hours and 80 km with a chauffeur-driven car: ₹2,500 sedan, ₹4,500 SUV, ₹7,500 Urbania or Tempo Traveller. Extra kilometres continue at the class rate, and tolls or parking are extra.

Can I book a one-way drop only?

Yes, and it is the cheaper option when you do not need the car back. One-way drops are billed on actual one-way distance, unlike operators who charge a return leg you never use.

How do I book a taxi?

Call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203 with pickup, drop, date, time and passenger count. You get a fixed written quote, pay a small advance, and receive driver and vehicle details before pickup.

How quickly can a car reach me in Sangam Vihar?

Usually 15–20 minutes for on-demand pickups, subject to live availability, because the dispatch desk sits inside Tigri Extension. Pre-booked slots are guaranteed ahead of on-demand requests.

Do you operate at night?

Phone and WhatsApp dispatch run 24x7. The walk-in counter is open 8 AM–10 PM daily. Night airport drops and late outstation departures should be booked at least a day ahead.

Can I schedule a pre-dawn airport pickup?

Yes. Book the previous day and the driver's name, vehicle number and live location are shared the evening before. The driver tracks your flight and adjusts to the actual landing or departure time.

What vehicle should I choose for a family of five?

An SUV — Ertiga or Innova Crysta at ₹24/km — gives six passenger seats plus boot space for luggage. For five passengers with heavy bags on a hill route, the Innova Crysta is the more comfortable assignment.

Do you provide child seats?

Tell the desk the child's age and weight at booking so the right seat can be arranged. Availability is confirmed in the written quote rather than assumed at pickup.

Which IGI terminal do you drop at?

The terminal printed on your ticket: T1, T2 or T3. If you are unsure, share the airline and flight number and the desk will confirm the terminal before the trip.

How does flight tracking work?

The desk monitors the flight number you provide. If the flight is delayed, the pickup window moves with the actual landing, and the 60-minute free waiting buffer starts from landing, not from the scheduled time.

What if my flight lands early?

The driver is dispatched against the flight, so an early landing is visible to the desk. If the car is still en route, you wait inside the arrivals hall and the driver meets you at the agreed pillar.

How much waiting time is free at the airport?

Sixty minutes after landing on both domestic and international arrivals. Beyond that, waiting is billed hourly at the rate stated in your written quote.

Do you cover international arrivals at Terminal 3?

Yes. T3 international arrivals are a standard rotation, with meet-and-greet at a pre-shared pillar, luggage assistance and a 60-minute free waiting buffer after landing.

Is a one-way outstation cheaper than a round trip?

For a single journey, yes: you pay one-way distance only. If you need the car at the destination or for the return, compare the round-trip quote — it usually prices better per kilometre than two one-way bookings.

How many days do I need for Vaishno Devi from Delhi?

Two driving days with a Jammu-side halt is the standard plan: leave early on day one, halt near Jammu, reach Katra next morning for the climb. Senior-friendly pacing adds a rest day on the return.

Which vehicle is best for Char Dham?

An SUV for clearance and comfort over 10–12 days, or a Tempo Traveller for larger groups. Assign drivers who run the circuit every season; mountain experience matters more than vehicle size.

Are permits arranged for interstate trips?

Yes. State permits, entry taxes and inter-state charges are arranged by the desk and billed at actuals with receipts, listed separately in the written quote.

Can you plan a Salasar and Khatu Shyam trip in one weekend?

Yes. The standard circuit is Salasar first, an overnight stay near Sikar, then Khatu Shyam the next morning before returning. The two temples are under 60 km apart.

Do you provide wheelchair or palki assistance for seniors?

Transport and halts are planned around mobility needs. Palki, pony and battery-car arrangements at the shrine are booked locally, and the desk coordinates the timing with your driver.

Is night driving part of outstation itineraries?

Only where the plan calls for it and the driver's rest window allows. On multi-day circuits a night halt is safer for passengers and crew than pushing through.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI and bank transfer on every trip. Corporate accounts can move to monthly consolidated invoicing after the first few runs, with every trip and receipt listed.

Will I get a receipt for tolls and parking?

Yes. The trip sheet lists fare, tolls, permits, parking and waiting separately, with the underlying receipts attached. Corporate accounts receive a consolidated monthly statement.

What is your cancellation policy?

Local and airport trips can be cancelled free up to four hours before pickup; outstation trips up to 24 hours before. Inside those windows the advance is adjusted against actual dispatch costs. The policy attached to your written quote governs.

Are your drivers verified?

Every chauffeur is background-checked and on a known roster rather than a rotating pool. Vehicles are GPS-tracked, and driver details are shared with you before pickup.

What happens if the car breaks down?

A replacement is dispatched from the nearest operational node and the original crew is recovered with priority. Waiting time caused by the breakdown is never re-billed to your trip.

How do I raise a complaint or correction?

Call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203 with the trip details. Billing disputes are checked against the trip sheet and receipts, and corrections to published content are applied to the page.
