How the estimator calculates your fare

  • Outstation mode multiplies the vehicle rate by distance: sedan ₹14/km, SUV ₹24/km, Urbania or Tempo Traveller ₹32/km.
  • Local mode starts from the 8-hour / 80-km package — ₹2,500 sedan, ₹4,500 SUV, ₹7,500 Urbania — then adds ₹14, ₹24 or ₹32 per kilometre beyond 80 km.
  • The distance slider runs from 50 to 1,000 km. For longer circuits, use the sample table below or ask the desk for a multi-day quote.
  • The total shown is the base fare, not the all-in trip cost. Tolls, permits and parking are added at actuals.

What the estimate does not include

  • Tolls, state permits, entry taxes and parking — billed at actuals with receipts
  • Waiting beyond the free airport buffer of 60 minutes after landing
  • Driver night-halt allowance on multi-day trips, shown in the written quote
  • Multiple pickup or drop points along the route
  • Vehicle class upgrades or a premium SUV such as the Fortuner, quoted on request

When the final quote differs from the estimate

The estimate assumes a direct point-to-point trip at the distance you set. A written quote can differ for four legitimate reasons, and each one is explained before the trip rather than after:

  • The route changes — a different pickup address, an added stop or a return leg
  • The trip needs waiting — a hospital OPD slot, a wedding function or a late flight
  • The corridor crosses a state border and needs a permit or entry tax
  • The trip runs multiple days and requires a driver night-halt

If the final bill ever exceeds the written quote without one of these causes, the desk corrects it. That is what the trip sheet and receipts are for.

Package or per-km: which is cheaper?

For a city day with several stops, the 8h/80km package is almost always cheaper than per-km billing. For a single airport run or a one-way intercity drop, per-km is the right model. The crossover is roughly 80 km of running in a day — below that the package wins on convenience, above it per-km keeps the cost honest.

Frequently asked questions

Is the fare estimator accurate?

It uses the exact rate card the dispatch desk quotes from: ₹14, ₹24 and ₹32 per kilometre, and ₹2,500, ₹4,500 and ₹7,500 for 8h/80km packages. The base fare is accurate; tolls and parking are added at actuals.

Why is the toll amount not included in the estimate?

Because tolls depend on the route actually driven and are billed at actuals with receipts. Folding them into the per-km rate would mean overcharging on some routes and undercharging on others.

Does the estimator include GST?

Rates shown are the transport fare. Applicable taxes, if any, are stated on the invoice and in the written quote — ask the desk to confirm the treatment for your booking type.

How do I book the fare I just estimated?

Tap 'Book This Trip on WhatsApp'. The message carries the journey type, vehicle class, distance and estimated fare to the dispatch desk, which replies with a fixed written quote.

Can I use the estimator for a round trip?

Set the slider to the round-trip distance, then ask the desk for a round-trip quote. Round trips often price better per kilometre than two one-way bookings, and the desk will tell you which is cheaper for your dates.

Want the number fixed in writing?

Send the estimate to the desk on WhatsApp and you will get a written quote with tolls and waiting itemised.
