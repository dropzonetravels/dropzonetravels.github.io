Frequently Asked Questions

Fares & pricing

How much is a taxi from Sangam Vihar to IGI Airport?＋

About 24 km. A sedan is roughly ₹336 at ₹14/km, an SUV ₹576 at ₹24/km and an Urbania or Tempo Traveller ₹768 at ₹32/km. Airport parking is billed at actuals. Call +91 72106 69203 for a fixed written quote.

How are outstation fares calculated?＋

Fare = vehicle class rate × distance. Sedan ₹14/km, SUV ₹24/km, Urbania and Tempo Traveller ₹32/km. One-way drops are billed on one-way distance, not a padded return leg. The quote you receive on WhatsApp is the number you pay.

Are tolls, permits and parking included in the fare?＋

No, and that is deliberate. Tolls, state permits, entry taxes and parking are billed at actuals with receipts attached to your trip sheet, so you pay the real ground cost instead of a padded package estimate.

What does a full-day local package cost?＋

₹2,500 for a sedan, ₹4,500 for an SUV and ₹7,500 for an Urbania or Tempo Traveller, each covering 8 hours and 80 km. Extra kilometres continue at ₹14, ₹24 or ₹32 per km depending on class.

Do you charge surge pricing or a night surcharge?＋

No. Rates do not move with demand, rain or the hour. Pre-dawn airport runs and late-night outstation departures are billed at the same published per-km rate as daytime trips.

Is the online fare estimator the final price?＋

The estimator gives your base contract fare from the same rate card we quote from. Tolls, permits, parking, waiting and driver night-halt (on multi-day trips) are listed separately and confirmed in writing before you pay.

Booking & dispatch

How do I book a taxi with Drop Zone Travels?＋

Call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203 with your pickup point, destination, date and time. You receive a fixed written quote, pay a small advance, and driver and vehicle details reach you before pickup.

How quickly can a cab reach me in Sangam Vihar?＋

The dispatch desk sits inside Tigri Extension, so pickups across Sangam Vihar pockets are usually assigned within 15–20 minutes, subject to live vehicle availability. Pre-booked slots are guaranteed ahead of on-demand requests.

Do you operate at night?＋

Phone and WhatsApp dispatch run 24x7. The walk-in counter at A-357, Mangal Bazaar Rd, Block A, Tigri Extension is staffed daily from 8 AM to 10 PM. Pre-dawn airport drops are booked a day ahead.

Can I schedule a pre-dawn IGI Airport pickup?＋

Yes. Pre-dawn airport runs are a standard rotation. Book the previous day and your driver's name, vehicle number and live location are shared the evening before, so there is no morning ambiguity.

Which vehicle should I choose?＋

Solo travellers and couples: sedan (Dzire or Aura, ₹14/km). Families with luggage or hill routes: SUV (Ertiga or Innova Crysta, ₹24/km). Groups of 9–17: Urbania or Tempo Traveller (₹32/km).

Can I book a one-way drop without a return trip?＋

Yes. One-way drops are our default for intercity travel and are quoted on the actual one-way distance. If you want the driver to wait or return with you, ask for a round-trip quote and compare.

Service area & trip types

Which areas do you serve?＋

All of Sangam Vihar and Tigri Extension, plus Deoli, Khanpur, Ambedkar Nagar, Dakshinpuri, Saket, Malviya Nagar, Chhattarpur, Kalkaji, Nehru Place and the wider South Delhi belt. Outstation and airport runs cover all of Delhi NCR.

Do you run outstation taxis to Jaipur, Agra and Chandigarh?＋

Yes. Jaipur, Agra, Chandigarh, Haridwar, Rishikesh, Shimla, Manali, Katra, Ayodhya, Amritsar and most North India corridors are routine. Distances, drive times and toll estimates are published in our route guides.

Can you manage a wedding fleet across multiple venues?＋

Yes. We run 5 to 40+ vehicle wedding grids with a dedicated coordinator on WhatsApp, guest shuttles, standby vehicles on rotation and nightly movement reporting for families and planners.

Do you provide tempo travellers and Urbania vans?＋

Yes. Force Urbania 9- and 12-seaters and Tempo Traveller 17-seaters at ₹32/km, or ₹7,500 for a full-day 8h/80km local package. They suit group pilgrimages, weddings, school trips and office outings.

Do you handle corporate and monthly car contracts?＋

Yes. Shift-rostered employee transport, dedicated monthly cars with one driver and consolidated monthly invoicing for offices across Nehru Place, Okhla, Gurugram and Noida.

Do you handle international arrivals at IGI Airport?＋

Yes. We track the flight, meet you at the pre-agreed arrivals pillar, handle luggage and hold a 60-minute free waiting buffer after landing. Return transfers to the airport are booked with the same desk.

Safety, payments & support

Are your drivers verified?＋

Every chauffeur is background-checked and on a known roster, not a rotating pool. Vehicles are GPS-tracked and the trip is logged with the dispatch desk for the duration of the run.

What happens if the vehicle breaks down mid-trip?＋

A replacement is dispatched from the nearest operational node, the original crew is recovered with priority, and waiting time caused by the breakdown is never re-billed to your trip.

What payment methods do you accept?＋

Cash, UPI and bank transfer on every trip. Corporate customers can move to monthly consolidated invoicing after the first few runs, with every trip listed against its receipts.

Will I get a receipt for tolls and parking?＋

Yes. Every trip closes with a line-item trip sheet showing fare, tolls, permits and parking separately, with the underlying receipts attached. Business accounts receive a consolidated monthly statement.

Is there a waiting charge at the airport?＋

Every airport pickup includes a 60-minute free waiting buffer after landing. Beyond that, waiting is billed hourly at a rate stated in your written quote before the trip.

How do I contact Drop Zone Travels?＋

Call or WhatsApp +91 72106 69203 any time. The office is at A-357, Mangal Bazaar Rd, Block A, Tigri Extension, Sangam Vihar, New Delhi, Delhi 110080, open daily 8 AM–10 PM.
