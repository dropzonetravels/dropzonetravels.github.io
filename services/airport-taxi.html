Which IGI terminal does your flight use?

Delhi airport splits airlines across three terminals, and pickups are mapped to the correct arrivals gate — tell us your terminal or flight number at booking and the desk handles the rest.

  • Terminal 1 — low-cost domestic carriers
  • Terminal 2 — select domestic operations
  • Terminal 3 — all international flights and major domestic carriers
  • Not sure which terminal? Send the flight number on WhatsApp and we map it for you

How an IGI pickup with Drop Zone Travels works

Four steps, zero waiting at the kerb:

  • Send your flight number, terminal, date and pickup address on WhatsApp or by phone
  • Receive a fixed written quote for your vehicle class — the fare does not change with traffic or hour
  • Get your driver's name, vehicle number and live location before you land
  • Meet at arrivals and roll out — fare, tolls and parking listed line-by-line on your trip sheet

Pre-booked airport taxi vs the airport app-cab queue

A pre-booked taxi and an on-demand app cab solve different problems. Here is where a booked vehicle wins for South Delhi travellers:

  • Fixed published rate vs demand-based surge pricing at peak landing hours
  • Vehicle confirmed before you board your flight vs waiting in the app-cab queue after a long flight
  • The same operator runs your return pickup to IGI — one desk, one number, consistent pricing
  • Line-item receipts that corporate travellers can attach to expense claims

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover all three IGI terminals?

Yes — Terminal 1, 2 and 3. Share your terminal or flight number at booking and the desk maps the correct arrivals gate; our drivers work all three terminals daily.

What does a Sangam Vihar to IGI T3 taxi cost?

Roughly ₹336 in a sedan (24 km × ₹14), ₹576 in an SUV and ₹768 in an Urbania or Tempo Traveller. Tolls and parking, if any, are billed at actuals with receipts — the written quote is the final figure.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when booking. The desk re-times your pickup to the revised landing slot, so your driver is at arrivals when you actually walk out — no rebooking needed.

Can I get an airport taxi at night?

Yes. Phone and WhatsApp dispatch runs 24x7, and the walk-in counter at A-357 Tigri Extension is open 8 AM–10 PM daily. For pre-dawn drops, reserve the slot a day ahead.

How much luggage fits in an airport taxi?

A sedan holds about two large check-in bags plus two cabin bags. Families with more luggage take an SUV; groups of 9–17 travel in an Urbania or Tempo Traveller.

Do you charge surge pricing?

No. Every airport run is billed at the published per-km rate for your vehicle class — the fare is the same at 5 AM and 5 PM.

How do I pay for my airport transfer?

Cash, UPI or bank transfer. Corporate travellers can request a consolidated invoice, and every trip sheet lists fare, tolls and parking separately.
