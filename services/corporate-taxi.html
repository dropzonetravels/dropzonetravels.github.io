How the corporate contract is set up

No long onboarding deck and no six-week pilot. The desk runs a short route survey and starts with a live roster:

  • Share pickup points, shift windows and daily headcount with the account coordinator
  • We map routes, assign fixed vehicles and chauffeurs, and share driver profiles with HR
  • Trips run against the roster with GPS tracking and a daily duty log
  • One consolidated invoice at month end, with every trip and receipt attached

Night shifts, women employees and safety protocol

Late shifts are where transport vendors are usually tested. Our protocol is written down and shared with your HR team before the first trip:

  • Driver and vehicle details messaged before every night leg
  • Live GPS link available to the employee and the admin desk
  • SOS escalation straight to the dispatch line, with a named account contact
  • Female attendants available on request for late-night routes

Corporate taxi vs app cabs for daily office transport

App cabs are fine for one-off errands. For a daily roster they create three hidden costs: surge on peak hours, a different driver every trip, and dozens of receipts for finance to reconcile. A monthly contract fixes the rate, keeps the same chauffeur on your route and collapses the paperwork into one invoice.

  • Predictable monthly cost instead of daily surge exposure
  • Fixed chauffeurs who know your pickup grid and building gates
  • GST-ready invoicing and a single reconciliation point
  • Replacement vehicle on service days, built into the contract

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide employee transport for night shifts?

Yes. Night rotations run with live GPS sharing, driver details sent before every leg and SOS escalation to the dispatch desk. Female attendants are available on request for late-night routes.

How is corporate transport billed?

Shared shift shuttles are billed per seat per month; dedicated cars per vehicle per month; ad-hoc runs per kilometre. Every model closes on one consolidated monthly invoice with trip logs attached.

Can you take over an existing employee transport roster?

Yes. Most contracts start with a route survey of your current pickup grid and shift timings, followed by a phased handover so employees are not disrupted during the switch.

Which office hubs do you cover?

Nehru Place, Okhla Phase 1–3, Saket, Malviya Nagar, Gurugram Cyber City, Noida Sector 62, Faridabad and the wider Delhi NCR belt, with South Delhi pickups dispatched from Tigri Extension.

Do you provide GPS tracking and duty logs?

Yes. Vehicles are GPS-tracked and the trip is logged against the roster. Your admin desk gets a daily duty log and a monthly summary with the invoice.

What vehicle classes are available for corporate use?

Sedans (Dzire, Aura), SUVs (Ertiga, Innova Crysta), Urbania 9/12-seaters and Tempo Traveller 17-seaters, plus Fortuner on request for senior leadership.

Is there a minimum contract size?

No fixed minimum. Small teams can start with a dedicated car; larger offices move to shift shuttles. The desk will tell you honestly which model is cheaper for your headcount and route.
