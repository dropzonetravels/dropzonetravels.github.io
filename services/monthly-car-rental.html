What a monthly car rental includes

The point of a monthly contract is that nothing surprising arrives at month end. The standard package covers:

  • One dedicated vehicle and chauffeur for your agreed daily route
  • Fuel and driver salary inside the fixed monthly price
  • Scheduled servicing, with a replacement vehicle on service days
  • A single invoice listing the month's trips and any actuals such as tolls or parking

Who a monthly car actually suits

Monthly rental is not cheaper than every alternative for every household — it wins where the route is predictable and the daily volume is real:

  • Families with school drops, tuition runs and office commutes on overlapping timings
  • NRI households managing elderly parents' hospital and temple visits from abroad
  • Small offices that need an executive car without buying one
  • Professionals with recurring client visits across Delhi NCR

Monthly contract vs daily app cabs

Do the arithmetic on your own month: daily app cab spend, surge on rainy days and peak hours, plus the time lost reconciling receipts. A monthly contract fixes the cost and the driver, which is usually the bigger win than the per-trip rate.

  • No surge exposure on peak-hour or monsoon days
  • Same chauffeur every day — knows the gates, the guards and the route
  • One invoice instead of dozens of trip receipts
  • Replacement vehicle built in, so a service day does not break your routine

Frequently asked questions

How much does a monthly car rental cost in Delhi?

It depends on daily distance, waiting windows and vehicle class, so the desk quotes after a one-call route survey. Fuel, driver and servicing are inside the fixed monthly price; tolls and parking stay at actuals.

Is fuel included in the monthly package?

Yes for the agreed daily route and kilometre band. Extra kilometres and outstation runs are quoted separately and shown on the invoice, never added silently.

What happens when the car is due for servicing?

A replacement vehicle is provided on service days as part of the contract. Your route continues without a gap and there is no extra charge for the swap.

Can I use the monthly car for outstation trips?

Yes, with the outstation leg quoted separately at the published per-km rate plus actuals. Tell the desk in advance so the driver's rest and night-halt plan is built into the roster.

Is a monthly car useful for NRI families with parents in Delhi?

It is one of our most common contracts. Parents get the same verified driver for hospital, temple and market runs, and family abroad gets a WhatsApp update after each trip.

Can the driver wait for appointments and school pickups?

Yes. Waiting windows are part of the route survey and the monthly quote. Tell us the longest regular wait — hospital OPDs and school gates are the usual ones — and it is priced in.

Do you require a deposit or long lock-in?

Monthly contracts run month to month with a small advance. There is no annual lock-in; the desk will confirm the exact terms in the written quote before you start.
