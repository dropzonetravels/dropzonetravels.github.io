Frequently asked questions

What is your outstation taxi fare per km?

Sedan ₹14/km, SUV ₹24/km and Urbania/Tempo Traveller ₹32/km. The fare is the class rate multiplied by trip distance — for example, Delhi to Jaipur in a sedan is about ₹3,920 (280 km × ₹14).

Do you charge for the return leg on one-way trips?

One-way drops are quoted on one-way distance at the published per-km rate. The full quote, including anything beyond distance, is confirmed in writing on WhatsApp before you pay.

Are tolls and state permits included?

No — tolls, state permits, parking and driver night-halt allowances are billed at actuals with receipts attached to your trip sheet, so you pay exactly what is charged on the ground.

How do I book an outstation taxi?

Call +91 72106 69203 or WhatsApp your pickup point, destination, date and time. You receive a fixed written quote, pay a small advance, and your driver and vehicle details reach you before departure.

Which vehicle should I take to the hills?

An SUV — Ertiga or Innova Crysta — is recommended for Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital and other hill corridors for power, grip and comfort on inclines. Sedans work well on plain corridors.

Can the driver halt overnight on multi-day trips?

Yes. Driver night-halt allowances are billed at actuals and confirmed in the written quote before the trip, so there are no surprise additions mid-journey.

How far in advance should I book?

Same-day bookings are usually possible on plains corridors. For weekends, festivals, hill seasons and group vehicles, reserve at least a day ahead.
