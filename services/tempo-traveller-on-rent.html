Frequently asked questions

What is the tempo traveller rate in Delhi?

₹32 per kilometre, chauffeur-driven, for the Urbania 9-seater, Urbania 12-seater and 17-seater Tempo Traveller. The full-day local package is ₹7,500 for 8 hours and 80 km.

How many seats does a tempo traveller have?

We run 9-seater and 12-seater Urbanias and a 17-seater Tempo Traveller. Larger wedding and event groups are deployed as multi-vehicle grids from the same dispatch desk.

Is the tempo traveller chauffeur-driven?

Yes — every group vehicle comes with a verified, background-checked driver, GPS tracking and dispatch coordination from our Tigri Extension base. Self-drive is available on select cars, not group vehicles.

Are the vehicles air-conditioned?

Yes, the Urbanias and Tempo Travellers run air-conditioned cabins as standard — a necessity for Delhi summers and long corridor runs.

How much luggage fits?

The rear bay carries luggage for a full group; oversized loads and equipment are confirmed with the desk at booking so the right layout is reserved.

How far in advance should I book a group vehicle?

Weekends, festival dates and wedding season book out fastest — reserve at least a few days ahead. Same-week bookings are often possible on quieter corridors; call the desk to check live availability.

Are tolls included in the ₹32/km rate?

No — tolls, state permits, parking and driver night-halt allowances are billed at actuals with receipts attached to your trip sheet, so you pay exactly what the ground charges are.
