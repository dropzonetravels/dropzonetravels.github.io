How a multi-venue wedding grid is planned

Most Delhi NCR weddings fail on transport because vehicles are booked function by function, not as one moving grid. We plan it as a single roster:

  • Venue-wise vehicle allocation with arrival buffers for baraat, pheras, reception and vidaai
  • Guest shuttles staged at hotels with a fixed departure loop instead of on-call pickups
  • Standby vehicles on rotation so a late function never strands the next one
  • One coordinator on WhatsApp who can move a vehicle between venues in minutes

What is included in the wedding package

The quote is fixed in writing before the first function, and it separates what we provide from what your vendors provide:

  • Vehicle, verified chauffeur, fuel and the agreed 8-hour / 80-km window per vehicle
  • Driver briefing on venue gates, parking routes and family contacts
  • Decoration coordinated with your decorator — the vehicle arrives clean and on time for fitting
  • Extra hours, extra kilometres, tolls and parking listed line-by-line on the final sheet

Guest shuttle math: how many vehicles do you actually need?

A 100-guest wedding with hotel-to-venue transfers usually needs two 17-seaters running a loop, plus one standby. A 250-guest function needs four to five shuttles and two standbys. Send the guest count, hotel list and function timings and the desk will build the roster and quote it vehicle by vehicle.

  • 9–12 guests per Urbania, 17 per Tempo Traveller for comfortable seating with luggage
  • Add one standby per 4–5 shuttles so a breakdown never breaks the loop
  • Keep the couple's car separate from guest shuttles — it should never be pulled into a shuttle run

Frequently asked questions

How many cars can you deploy for a wedding?

We scale from 5 to 40+ vehicles with a dedicated coordinator, staged guest shuttles and standby rotation. Send the guest count, venues and function timings for a vehicle-wise roster and quote.

Can you provide a decorated car for the couple?

Yes. The sedan or SUV is supplied clean and on time for your decorator to fit. Decoration itself is handled by your vendor; we coordinate the fitting window so the car is ready before the baraat.

Do you cover weddings outside Delhi?

Yes — Jaipur, Agra, Chandigarh and other North India destinations are routine. Outstation weddings are quoted with driver night-halt and tolls at actuals, itemised in writing.

What happens if a vehicle breaks down during a function?

A standby vehicle is already staged on rotation at multi-vehicle weddings. If a vehicle fails, the replacement moves in and the waiting time is never re-billed.

Are guest shuttles billed per trip or per day?

Shuttles are billed on the 8-hour / 80-km package per vehicle, with extra hours and kilometres quoted upfront. For multi-day weddings we quote each day separately so there are no surprises.

Can we book only the couple's car?

Yes. Single-car bookings are welcome at the standard package rate. Most families add at least one guest shuttle once they see the per-vehicle math.

How far in advance should we book wedding transport?

Reserve four to six weeks ahead for peak wedding season (November–February), and earlier for multi-city functions. Vehicle availability tightens sharply on auspicious dates.
