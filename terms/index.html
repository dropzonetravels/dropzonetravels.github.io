Terms of Service – Bookings, Fares and Trip Rules

Drop Zone Travels · A-357, Mangal Bazaar Rd, Block A, Tigri Extension, Sangam Vihar, New Delhi, Delhi 110080 · +91 72106 69203

Last updated: 15 November 2026. These terms apply to bookings made by phone, WhatsApp or in person with Drop Zone Travels, A-357, Mangal Bazaar Rd, Block A, Tigri Extension, Sangam Vihar, New Delhi 110080.

The written quote issued for your trip, and the cancellation terms attached to it, form part of these terms. Where the quote is more specific than this page, the quote governs.

These terms are an operational summary written for riders. They are not legal advice, and the written quote for your trip may add trip-specific conditions.

Quotes and confirmation

  • A quote is an offer for the trip described in it — route, dates, vehicle class and inclusions.
  • A booking is confirmed when you accept the quote and pay any advance stated in it.
  • Changes to route, dates, stops or vehicle class are re-quoted before they are driven.
  • Quotes do not include tolls, permits, entry taxes, parking, waiting beyond the stated free window, or driver night-halt unless listed.

Fares and billing

  • Per-km fares are calculated at the published class rate: sedan ₹14/km, SUV ₹24/km, Urbania or Tempo Traveller ₹32/km.
  • Full-day local packages cover 8 hours and 80 km; extra kilometres continue at the class rate.
  • One-way trips are billed on one-way distance, with no return-leg charge.
  • Tolls, permits and parking are billed at actuals with receipts attached to the trip sheet.
  • Waiting is free for 60 minutes after landing on airport pickups and otherwise as stated in the quote.
  • The balance is payable at the end of the trip unless the quote states otherwise.

Vehicles, drivers and capacity

  • The vehicle class is confirmed in writing before pickup. Registration numbers may change where a replacement vehicle of the same class is assigned.
  • Passenger seats are as stated for the class, excluding the driver. Luggage space is limited and must be declared at booking.
  • Drivers are assigned from a verified roster and their details are shared before pickup.
  • The driver may refuse a trip where the passenger count, luggage or conduct makes the journey unsafe or unlawful.

Passenger responsibilities

  • Provide accurate pickup and drop addresses and be ready at the agreed time.
  • Do not carry prohibited or hazardous items, or exceed the vehicle's legal capacity.
  • Wear seat belts where fitted, and follow the driver's instructions on safety.
  • Smoking, alcohol and abusive conduct are not permitted in the vehicle.
  • Damage to the vehicle caused by a passenger may be charged at the actual repair cost.

Delays, breakdowns and alternatives

Traffic, weather, road closures and government restrictions can affect journey times. We plan realistic windows and communicate changes, but we do not guarantee arrival times against conditions outside our control.

  • If a vehicle breaks down, a replacement is dispatched from the nearest operational node.
  • Waiting time caused by a breakdown is not re-billed to the trip.
  • Where a replacement cannot reach you within a reasonable time, the unused portion of the fare is refunded.
  • Flight delays are handled against the actual landing time where flight tracking was requested.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation terms depend on trip type and are attached to the written quote. The current policy is published on the cancellation policy page, which forms part of these terms. Refunds are returned by the original payment method after the trip record is checked.

Liability

  • We are responsible for providing the vehicle and driver described in the confirmed booking.
  • We are not liable for losses caused by events outside our control, including traffic, weather, strikes, road closures and government action.
  • Passengers are responsible for personal belongings. Items left in a vehicle are held for collection where possible but are not insured.
  • Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Governing law and contact

These terms are governed by the laws of India, with courts in Delhi having jurisdiction. Questions about a booking, a quote or these terms should go to +91 72106 69203 — the same number handles bookings, billing queries and complaints.
